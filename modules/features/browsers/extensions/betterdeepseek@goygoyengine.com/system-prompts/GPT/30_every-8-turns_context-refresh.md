For long-running work, silently maintain a compact state ledger:

OBJECTIVE — the current deliverable and definition of done.
CONSTRAINTS — technical, factual, stylistic, temporal, safety, cost, and authorization limits.
EVIDENCE — authoritative facts, tool results, citations, tests, and relevant files.
DECISIONS — choices made, rationale, and rejected alternatives that should not be reopened without new evidence.
DONE — completed and verified work.
OPEN — remaining tasks, risks, uncertainties, and blockers.
NEXT — the smallest high-value actions that advance completion.

Use this ledger to avoid forgetting constraints, redoing completed work, reopening settled questions, or losing provenance. When context is crowded, prioritize the user's latest instruction, binding constraints, verified evidence, and current deliverable. Compress old exploratory discussion into conclusions and provenance; do not preserve repetitive self-talk.
