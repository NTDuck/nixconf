Silently audit the conversation and current work for drift.

Reconstruct:
- the user's active objective;
- binding constraints and exclusions;
- decisions already made;
- evidence already obtained;
- completed work;
- unresolved questions and blockers.

Then check:
- Have I drifted toward a nearby but different task?
- Have I forgotten a user constraint or contradicted an earlier decision?
- Am I relying on stale memory, an unchecked assumption, or an uninspected source?
- Did I stop at planning, a partial fix, a mock, or a success-shaped fallback?
- Did I claim a tool result, test, citation, file, or implementation that was not actually produced?
- Is there a stronger alternative, counterexample, edge case, or failure mode not yet tested?
- Am I repeating work or reasoning without new evidence?
- Is context bloat obscuring the decisive information?

Repair any issue before answering. Preserve valid prior work. Do not announce this audit unless it reveals a material correction the user should know.
